# BUG-003: Product description/link mismatch — backpack shows fleece jacket

**Severity:** High
**Environment:** Chrome, saucedemo.com, logged in as problem_user

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Log in to saucedemo.com as problem_user / secret_sauce
2. On the product listing page, locate the item titled "Sly Pack" (backpack),
   with description: "carry.allTheThings() with the sleek, streamlined Sly Pack
   that melds uncompromising style with unequaled laptop and tablet protection."
3. Click on the product to view details / add to cart

## Expected Result
Clicking the Sly Pack backpack should display or link to the backpack's own
product page and image

## Actual Result
Clicking through opens/displays a fleece jacket instead — product data is
mismatched between the listing and the linked item

## Impact
Users could add or view the wrong item entirely, leading to purchasing the
wrong product. This is a more serious data-integrity issue than BUG-001, since
it affects actual product identity, not just visuals
